Privacy Policy

Summary

Card Check does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your card photos are analyzed entirely on your device and are never uploaded. The app does connect to public card-pricing services to look up market values — those requests contain only card search terms, never anything that identifies you.

What we don't collect

  • No name, email, address, phone number, or any other personal information
  • No device identifiers (no IDFA, no advertising ID)
  • No analytics, crash reports, telemetry, or usage statistics
  • No location data
  • No advertising. No third-party tracking SDKs. No tracking pixels.

Your photos stay on your device

When you scan or choose a card photo, Card Check measures its centering and reads any printed text using Apple's on-device Vision framework. This all happens locally on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we never store them.

Network use (card pricing)

To show market values, Card Check sends a card's name and collector number — typed by you, or read from the photo on your device — to the public pricing service pokemontcg.io, which returns prices and a card image. When you tap a price link, the app opens TCGplayer, Cardmarket, or eBay in your browser to view live or sold listings.

These requests contain only the card search terms. They do not include your name, account, location, device identifiers, or any other personal information. We do not log or store these lookups. The third-party services you connect to (pokemontcg.io, TCGplayer, Cardmarket, eBay) operate under their own privacy policies.

What is stored on your device

Card Check keeps only standard iOS app state and temporary image caches in the app's private container on your device. Nothing is synced or transmitted. Deleting the app erases this data permanently.

Children

The app is rated 4+. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, the app complies with COPPA, GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy regulations by virtue of collecting nothing in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new revision date.
